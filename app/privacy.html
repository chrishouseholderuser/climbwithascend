Privacy

Plain-language privacy for unofficial Digital SAT® exam practice. No ads. We do not sell student data.

Who this is for

Ascend is for students 13 and older. We do not collect a birthdate. Creating an account requires confirming you are 13 or older.

What we collect

  • Account details you choose to give us, such as email and a username, so you can sign in.
  • Climb progress you create in the app (practice history, goals, and similar game data) so it can sync to your account.
  • Basic technical logs that help keep the site running.
  • Anonymous usage counts: a random id on your device, plus whether you started a climb and whether you answered a question recently. We use this to see if the free site is being used. No name, no email, no ads, and we do not sell this.

What we do not do

  • No ads.
  • We do not sell student data.
  • We do not ask for a birthdate.
  • Passwords are handled by our sign-in provider. This app does not store your password.

How sync works

If you create an account, your progress can sync so you can keep climbing on more than one device. You can log out anytime from the Dashboard.

Unofficial practice

Ascend is unofficial practice. Climb progress is not an official SAT® exam score. Use Bluebook™ for official practice tests.
