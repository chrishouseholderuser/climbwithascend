Students never pay

I built Ascend so a kid never hits a checkout just to practice.

My promise

I will not charge students for Ascend. Not for the daily climb, not for lessons, not for the unofficial practice test. I will not put a paywall in front of someone who is trying to prepare.

If this grows

If Ascend takes off, I may ask schools for an optional teacher dashboard, or let donors and parents support the project. That money will never unlock questions that were already free. It is optional for adults and schools. It is never required for a student to climb.

What I will not do

  • No student Pro tier that locks practice behind a monthly fee.
  • No ads on the climb.
  • No selling student data.

Who I am

I'm Chris Householder. I built this. I want every climber to have a fair shot.

Unofficial

Ascend is unofficial practice for the Digital SAT® exam. If you create an account, you need to be 13 or older.

Talk to me

If this looks unclear, email me at hello@climbwithascend.org.
