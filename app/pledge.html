Forever Free Pledge

Core practice stays free. No student paywall. Built by a student with help from his dad.

What stays free forever

  • Core practice — the daily climb, lessons, and skill quizzes.
  • Unofficial full-length practice — the long adaptive practice exam on this site.
  • Parent view — a way for a parent or trusted adult to see progress without paying.

No student paywall

We will not lock the practice a student needs behind a paywall. Ascend is meant to be a free climb, not a product that charges kids to study.

Who built this

Built by a student with help from his dad. We want every climber to have a fair shot.

Unofficial and ad-free

Ascend is unofficial practice for the Digital SAT® exam. No ads. We do not sell student data. Students must be 13 or older.

Contact

If this pledge ever looks unclear, contact the site owner at hello@climbwithascend.org.
