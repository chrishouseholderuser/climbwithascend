The promise

Students never pay for Ascend. Not for the daily climb, not for lessons, not for the unofficial practice test. We will not put a paywall in front of a teenager who is trying to prepare.

How we may keep the lights on

If Ascend grows, we may ask schools for an optional teacher dashboard, or ask donors and parents to support the project. That money never unlocks questions a student already had for free. It is optional for adults and institutions. It is never required for a student to climb.

What we will not do

  • No student Pro tier that locks practice behind a monthly fee.
  • No ads on the student climb.
  • No selling student data.

Who built this

Built by a student with help from his dad. We want every climber to have a fair shot.

Unofficial

Ascend is unofficial practice for the Digital SAT® exam. Students creating an account must be 13 or older.
