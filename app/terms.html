Terms

Simple rules for using Ascend, a free unofficial practice site.

The short version

Ascend is free unofficial practice for the Digital SAT® exam. It is not the College Board, and the College Board does not endorse this site.

Who may use Ascend

Ascend is for students 13 and older. You must confirm that age when you create an account. Do not create an account for a child under 13.

Unofficial

Practice numbers and climb progress here are unofficial. They are not official SAT® exam scores. Take a Bluebook™ practice test before test day if you want an official practice result.

Your account

Keep your password private. You are responsible for activity on your account. Do not try to break or misuse the site.

Content

Some unofficial practice items come from open sources such as OpenSAT. They are not official College Board items. Lessons, design, and the climb experience are part of Ascend.

No ads, no student paywall

Students never pay for practice. We do not run ads on this site.

Contact

Contact the site owner at hello@climbwithascend.org.
